What makes Meadow different

A paragraph, a shape, a sticky note and a pen stroke are all the same kind of thing here: an object on the canvas. These are the parts you will not find in most whiteboards, and every one of them below is live.

Thirteen Indian languages

Spell a word the way it sounds in English letters and take it in its own script. No keyboard layout to install, on any board.
