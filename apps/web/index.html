Open Meadow

One endless canvas for writing, drawing and thinking together

Meadow is an open-source infinite canvas whiteboard. Prose, diagrams, sketches and sticky notes share a single surface with no page edge, and everyone on a board sees every change as it happens.

Open Meadow See what it does

A whiteboard where everything is the same kind of thing

A paragraph, a rectangle, an arrow, a sticky note and a pen stroke are all just objects at a position. You move, label, connect, restack and share them without switching modes or opening a second app.

Diagram with shapes that hold their text

Eight primitives — rectangle, ellipse, diamond, parallelogram, triangle, trapezoid, polygon and cylinder — each on its own key. Every one carries its own label, so a box with text is one object, not two that drift apart.

Arrows that stay attached

Connectors come straight, curved or elbowed and bind to shapes rather than to coordinates. Move a shape and its arrows follow, resolved against the real outline instead of a bounding box.

Real rich text on the canvas

Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and eight sizes, edited in place at any zoom. Two people typing in one paragraph merge character by character.

Draw with pressure

Five nibs — ballpoint, fineliner, calligraphy, brush and highlighter — in four widths and seven colours. A stylus's pressure is honoured, and a mouse's speed stands in for it, so a mouse-drawn line still reads as handwriting.

Type in your own script

Phonetic input for thirteen languages of India: Bengali, Hindi, Assamese, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Tamil and Telugu. Type amar, get আমার — no keyboard layout to install.

A ruled page, when you want one

A lea is a printed surface rather than a blank one: ruled lines, a subject and a date, and pages you can add, name, reorder and tear out. Same document, same tools, different paper.

Real-time collaboration that survives a closed laptop

Open a board with somebody and you see their cursor, their selection and their edits as they happen. Work offline and the two versions merge when you come back, rather than asking which one to keep. Meadow syncs with CRDTs, so there is no "resolve conflict" step and no last-writer-wins overwrite.

Share by link or by address

A board is restricted until you say otherwise. Turn on the link and anyone holding it can open the board, signed in or not, as a viewer or an editor. Invitations work whether or not the address has an account yet.

Access that changes immediately

Roles resolve from the database on every request and every socket connect, so removing somebody closes their board instead of waiting for a token to expire.

Every session, visible

Your profile lists every browser holding a session — what it is, from where, and when it was last active. Terminate one and that browser is locked out at once.

Open source, and yours to host

Meadow runs on your own server with Docker Compose. Your boards, your database, your infrastructure — no seat count and no vendor holding the data. Sign in with an email and password, with GitHub or with Google; all three land on one account keyed by the verified address.

Questions

What is an infinite canvas?

An infinite canvas is a workspace with no page edge and no fixed size. You pan and zoom instead of scrolling to the bottom of a document, and a diagram, a paragraph and a sketch can sit side by side at whatever scale suits them.

Can several people edit a Meadow board at the same time?

Yes. Meadow is live by default: you see other people's cursors, selections and edits as they happen. Two people typing in the same paragraph merge character by character rather than overwriting each other.

Does Meadow work offline?

Yes. Edits are stored locally and merged when you reconnect, so closing the laptop mid-thought does not lose work and does not force anyone to choose which version to keep.

Is Meadow open source and can I self-host it?

Yes. Meadow runs on your own server with Docker Compose, so your boards stay on infrastructure you control.

Can I type in Indian languages on a Meadow board?

Yes. Meadow has phonetic input for thirteen languages of India, including Bengali, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi, Assamese, Nepali and Sanskrit. You type amar and get আমার, with no keyboard layout to install.
